The short version

TENDS Journal is a private feeding journal for your dog. Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up. We don't show ads and we don't sell your data. Analytics can be turned off at any time. You can delete everything from inside the app.

What the app stores, and where

DataExamplesWhere it lives

The app does not collect your contacts, location, messages, financial details, or your device's advertising ID.

Analytics

The welcome screen asks you to agree to anonymous usage analytics. You can turn analytics off at any time in the app: open Profile (tap your dog's picture on Today), then "Help improve the app". Collection stops when you do.

Cloud backup (optional)

Backup is off unless you sign in with Google in Profile. When it is on:

  • Your journal is stored in our cloud database (Supabase) against your account.
  • Access is restricted so that only your own account can read your data.
  • Photos are stored privately and shown to you through short-lived links.

If you never turn on backup, your journal never leaves your phone.

The numbers in the app

The daily amount and other figures are estimates worked out on your phone from what you enter, using published methods. They are not a veterinary assessment. The app records what you observe; it does not diagnose. Please talk to your vet about your dog's diet and health.

Sharing

When you export a record for your vet or share a photo card, the file is made on your phone and handed to your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes. We receive nothing from it.

Services we use

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and storage for backup (only if you turn on backup).
  • Google Sign-In: to sign in for backup.
  • PostHog: usage analytics and anonymised session recordings, while analytics are on. posthog.com/privacy
  • Microsoft Clarity: anonymised tap heatmaps, while analytics are on. Microsoft privacy statement
  • Expo: delivers app updates. It does not receive your journal.

These providers process data on our behalf to run the service. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Delete your data or your account

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open TENDS Journal and tap your dog's picture on Today to open Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my data.
  3. Confirm with Delete everything.

This permanently deletes your dog's profile, plan, journal notes and photos from your phone and, if backup was on, from our cloud. It cannot be undone.

Delete your account by email

To also delete the sign-in account linked to your Google email, or if you no longer have the app, email chan@tendsmall.com with the subject "TENDS Journal account deletion", from the email you signed in with. We delete the account and all data linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: dog profile, plan, journal notes, photos, and the backup copy of all of these; on request, your sign-in account (email and name).
  • Kept: nothing from your journal. Anonymous usage analytics that cannot be linked back to you may remain in our analytics providers under their own retention periods.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the UK or the EU), you may have the right to access, correct or erase your data. Email chan@tendsmall.com and we will help.

Children

TENDS Journal is for adult dog owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app for important changes.
