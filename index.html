TENDS Journal

Privacy policy and data deletion

How the TENDS Journal app handles your data, and how to delete it.

Last updated: 28 November 2026
App: TENDS Journal (Android, package com.tendsmall.core), developer "tendsmall" on Google Play
Data controller: Blue ThinQ Ltd, United Kingdom, the company that publishes TENDS Journal
Contact: hello@tendsmall.com

The short version

TENDS Journal is a feeding journal for your dog. Your journal is on your phone, and once you agree in the app, TENDS keeps a copy on its server so it is safe if your phone is lost and so we can help you with feeding. Photos stay on your phone, unless you join the TENDS Fed First cohort and agree to share them. We don't show ads and we don't sell your data. Analytics can be turned off at any time. You can delete everything from inside the app.

Analytics

The welcome screen asks two separate questions, and both are optional: whether TENDS may keep a copy of your journal, and whether to share anonymous usage analytics. The app works the same either way. You can turn analytics off at any time in the app: open Profile (tap your dog's picture on Today), then "Help improve the app". Collection stops when you do.

The copy of your journal TENDS keeps

From 20 November 2026 the app asks once (on the welcome screen, or with a one-time notice if you started earlier) whether TENDS may keep a copy of your journal. Once you agree:

  • Your dog's profile, the feeding plan and your journal notes, including what you write, are saved in our database (Supabase, servers in Singapore) and kept up to date as you add notes.
  • The copy sits under an anonymous account made by the app, with no name or email, unless you choose to sign in.
  • Who sees it. The TENDS team: Blue ThinQ Ltd, Tendsmall Ltd in the UK and Kariva Plant Bakes in India, who make TENDS and run the cohort with us. Nobody else.
  • Your phone can only read and change its own copy. TENDS uses the copies to keep your journal safe, to see how feeding with TENDS is going so we can help you, and to improve our food and the app.
  • Photos are not copied, unless you joined the cohort and agreed (see above). Leaving the cohort or switching the copy off deletes them from our server. Photos backed up by an earlier version of the app (0.3.1 or before) stay in your private backup until you delete your data.
  • How long we keep it. For as long as you use the app. If the app isn't opened for 24 months, we delete our copy. Deleted data leaves our backups within 30 days.
  • Stopping the copy. You can stop it at any time: open Profile, then switch off "A copy of your dog's journal". We delete our copy straight away. The journal on your phone stays.

The numbers in the app

The daily amount and other figures are estimates worked out on your phone from what you enter, using published methods. They are not a veterinary assessment. The app records what you observe; it does not diagnose. Please talk to your vet about your dog's diet and health.

Sharing

When you export a record for your vet or share a photo card, the file is made on your phone and handed to your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes. We receive nothing from it.

Services we use

  • Supabase: the database and sign-in that hold the copy of your journal and, for the cohort, photos. Servers in Singapore.
  • Apple and Google: optional, only if you choose to sign in with them. They tell us your email address.
  • PostHog: counts which screens are opened and which buttons are tapped, while analytics are on. Servers in the European Union. posthog.com/privacy
  • Microsoft Clarity: tap heatmaps and screen recordings with every word and image hidden, while analytics are on. Microsoft privacy statement
  • Expo: delivers app updates. Your phone asks it for the latest version, which shares the app version, the type of phone and, like any web request, its internet address. It does not receive your journal.
  • Shopify: runs the TENDS store. When you enter a TENDS code, the app checks it with the store. Only the code is sent, nothing about you or your dog.

These providers process data on our behalf to run the service. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Delete your data or your account

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open TENDS Journal and tap your dog's picture on Today to open Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my data.
  3. Confirm with Delete everything.

This permanently deletes your dog's profile, plan, journal notes and photos from your phone and our copy on the server. It cannot be undone.

After this the app starts again from the welcome screen.

Delete your account

To also delete your sign-in account, or if you no longer have the app, email hello@tendsmall.com with the subject "TENDS Journal account deletion", from the email you signed in with. We delete the account and all data linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: dog profile, plan, journal notes, photos, and our copy of all of these; on request, your sign-in account and its email.
  • Kept: nothing from your journal. Anonymous usage analytics that cannot be linked back to you may remain in our analytics providers under their own retention periods.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the UK or the EU), you may have the right to access, correct or erase your data. Email hello@tendsmall.com and we will help. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Children

TENDS Journal is for adult dog owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app for important changes.
